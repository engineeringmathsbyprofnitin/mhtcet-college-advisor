# Stage 12c-5 - NAAC "Transition Autonomous Colleges" sheet

Why: the card said "No NAAC record found" for colleges that NAAC does list on the second sheet of the owner's Excel (autonomous colleges whose accreditation was extended; no grade). Example: K J Somaiya Institute of Technology (NAAC name: K J Somaiya Institute of Engineering and Information Technology, Sion East), extended until 31 Dec 2026.
- `ingestion/naac_import.py` also reads the sheet (Maharashtra rows, 113) into `data/normalized/naac/naac_transition.csv` (name = text before "(Autonomous" or the first comma, full text kept, extended_until as ISO date; dates accepted as Excel numbers, dd/mm/yyyy, dd-mm-yyyy and the typo 31/122025; an unreadable date is a problem).
- `ingestion/naac_match.py` (`build_transition`): colleges WITHOUT a graded record are matched with the same strict rules to those rows -> `data/normalized/naac_link/naac_transition_match.csv`. Owner/assistant answers: `data/manual/naac_transition_confirmations.csv` (college_code, source_row, decision). Result: 17 colleges linked (8 automatic, 9 decided by the assistant because name and address agree: 02113 Maharashtra IT Aurangabad, 03139 Vidyalankar, 03176 Thakur, 04136 Priyadarshini J.L., 04137 S.B. Jain, 04138 J D College, 05160 Sanjivani, 06175 PCCOE Nigdi, 06268 Tatyasaheb Kore), the other 22 doubtful pairs were set to "no" (for example the Raisoni row has no address, so it is not given to any of the three Raisoni colleges).
- `ingestion/build_accreditation.py`: a transition college gets a NAAC row with grade and CGPA empty, valid_to = the extended date, listed_as_on = 2025-08-14. A college never has both a graded and a transition row. 1,454 rows in all (NBA 1,204, NAAC graded 233, NAAC transition 17).
- Engine: a row with a date and no grade is never scored (ranking unchanged). Explanation: "NAAC: accreditation extended until 31 Dec 2026 (autonomous college in NAAC's transition list; grade not stated); list as on 14 Aug 2025." or "...extended validity ended on 31 Dec 2024...".
- Card: "Accreditation extended until 31 Dec 2026" (or grey "Extended validity ended on 31 Dec 2024") + "Autonomous college in NAAC's transition list; grade not stated" + "On NAAC's list as on 14 Aug 2025".
- Checked on packaged real data: K J Somaiya Institute of Technology and Vidyalankar show "extended until"; Thakur College shows the grey "ended" text.
