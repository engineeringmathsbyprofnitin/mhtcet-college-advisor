1. Unzip `mhtcet-stage9a.zip`.
2. In your repository click **Add file -> Upload files** and drag in the folders `ingestion`, `tests`, `data` and `docs`. Click **Commit changes**. (The `data` folder adds `data/normalized/cutoffs_mh/` with 7 small compressed files and a report.)
3. To see the verification, open `data/normalized/cutoffs_mh/parse_report_mh.json` on GitHub. For each of the 7 lists it shows `"complete": true` and `"problem_count": 0`.
