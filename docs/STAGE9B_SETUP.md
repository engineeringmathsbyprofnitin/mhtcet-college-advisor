# Stage 9b: put the results into your repository (optional now, about 5 minutes)

Nothing needs to be run: the reading was done and verified here from your archived files. This zip is cumulative: it contains everything from 9a as well, so you do not need the 9a zip. You may also wait and upload once after 9c.

1. Unzip `mhtcet-stage9b.zip`.
2. In your repository click **Add file -> Upload files** and drag in the folders `ingestion`, `tests`, `data` and `docs`. Click **Commit changes**. (Same-named files are replaced.)
3. To see the verification, open `data/normalized/cutoffs_ai/parse_report_ai.json` and `data/normalized/cutoffs_mh/parse_report_mh.json`. Every list shows `"complete": true` and `"problem_count": 0`.
